# Brand Central lobby assets

`atrium_landscape.png` and `atrium_portrait.png` are separately composed generated architectural background plates, created with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-26. They are not extracted game footage. The app adds its own map and interactive UI; the camera drift is a Flutter animation, not a video of moving escalator steps.

Prompt: realistic warm limestone atrium, two black escalators with glass and chrome railings, skylight, cream polished floor, symmetrical front architectural photography, softly defocused, no people, text, logos, maps, cars or UI. Landscape 16:9 and a dedicated portrait 9:16 recomposition of the same setting.

The four map SVGs derive from Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 Countries, equirectangular projection with Antarctica omitted. Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson . Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Regional overlays are geographic highlights, not sales territories.

Composition reference: official GT7 manual https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/manual/brandcentral/01 and its linked region/manufacturer screenshots. Game UI and logos are not baked into the generated background.
